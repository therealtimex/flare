Flare's logo: the lockup (wordmark plus signal disc), the mark (funnel F plus disc), and the app icon.

- `flare-lockup.svg`, `flare-mark.svg`: ink #171717 (light `ink`) with the #ff5a1f `flare` disc. Use on light grounds.
- `flare-lockup-reverse.svg`, `flare-mark-reverse.svg`: ink #f5f5f5 (dark `ink`) with the `flare` disc. Use on dark `surface-0`.
- `flare-app-icon.svg`: the reverse mark on a #0a0a0a tile, corner radius 7/32. Favicons, app and social avatars.
- The wordmark is Geist SemiBold converted to outlines, so it renders without the font.
- Minimum sizes: lockup 72px wide, mark 16px. Clear space: one disc diameter on every side.
- The disc is always `flare`. Never recolor it or place the logo on an orange ground.
